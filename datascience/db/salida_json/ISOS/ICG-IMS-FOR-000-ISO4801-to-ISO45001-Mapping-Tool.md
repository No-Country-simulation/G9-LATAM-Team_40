|**HOW TO USE THIS TOOL**|This mapping guide is designed to help organisations transition from AS/NZS 4801:2001 to ISO45001:2018 with clarity and confidence.| ||1. Identify the ISO 45001 clause you need to address in your safety management system.2. Check the equivalent AS/NZS 4801 clause listed in the table. If an exact match exists, you canadapt your existing processes and documents.3. Note "no direct equivalent" clauses — these highlight new ISO 45001 requirements that mayrequire fresh procedures, risk registers, or documented evidence.4. Pay attention to partial matches — these may require expanding your current controls or addingnew elements (e.g., context analysis, worker participation, managing risks and opportunities).5. Use the Q&A format as a quick reference in toolbox meetings, internal training, and implementationplanning.6. Integrate changes into your management system so they align with the Plan–Do–Check–Act cycleand support continual improvement.This tool is not a replacement for the standards themselves — always refer to the full text of ISO 45001and AS/NZS 4801 for detailed requirements.|
