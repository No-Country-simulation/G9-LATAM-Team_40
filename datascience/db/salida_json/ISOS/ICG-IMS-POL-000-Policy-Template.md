# ICG-IMS-POL-000-Policy-Template

### POLICY TEMPLATE ICG-IMS-POL-000

This Integrated Management System (IMS) Policy sets out the commitment of [Organisation Name] to quality, environmental management, and work health and safety in accordance with the requirements of:

- ISO 9001:2015 – Quality Management Systems - ISO 14001:2015 – Environmental Management Systems - ISO 45001:2018 – Occupational Health and Safety Management Systems This policy applies to all workers, officers, contractors and other relevant interested parties engaged in activities under the control or influence of [Organisation Name], across all locations and operations.

We are committed to:

- Delivering products and services that consistently meet customer, statutory and regulatory requirements.

- • Understanding and meeting the needs and expectations of our customers and other relevant interested parties.

- • Protecting the environment, including prevention of pollution and responsible use of resources.

- Identifying and managing environmental aspects and impacts associated with our activities, products and services.

- Meeting applicable environmental legal and other compliance obligations.

- Providing safe and healthy working conditions to prevent work-related injury and ill health.

- Eliminating hazards and reducing WHS risks so far as is reasonably practicable.

achieves its intended outcomes.

Managers and Supervisors are responsible for implementing this policy within their areas of control.

Workers and Contractors are responsible for complying with this policy, procedures, and participating in consultation and improvement activities.
