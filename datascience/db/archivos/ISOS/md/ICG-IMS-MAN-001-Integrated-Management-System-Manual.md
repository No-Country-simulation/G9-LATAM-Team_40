## INTEGRATED MANAGEMENT SYSTEM MANUAL

## 1 Scope ### 1.1 General This document outlines the overall structure and processes that form Company's Integrated Management System (IMS). This IMS is designed to meet the requirements of the AS/NZS ISO 45001:2018, 9001:2016 and 14001:2016. In particular, the system aims to meet the intended outcomes listed below:

- Continual improvement of HSEQ performance,

- Achievement of HSEQ objectives.

### 1.2 Plan-Do-Check-Act Methodology Company uses risk and evidence-based thinking and a process-based IMS that incorporates the PlanDo-Check-Act (PDCA) methodology as described below.
