Identify the ISO 45001 clause you need to address in your safety management system.<br>2. Check the equivalent AS/NZS 4801 clause listed in the table. Note "no direct equivalent" clauses — these highlight new ISO 45001 requirements that may<br>require fresh procedures, risk registers, or documented evidence.<br>4. Pay attention to partial matches — these may require expanding your current controls or adding<br>new elements (e.g., context analysis, worker participation, managing risks and opportunities).<br>5.
